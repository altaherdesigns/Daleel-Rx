Daleel Rx is a medicine reference for pharmacists working in the United Arab Emirates. It is built and reviewed by **Muaaz Butt**, a pharmacist licensed by the UAE Ministry of Health and Prevention (Northern Emirates) and the Dubai Health Authority, based in Fujairah.

دليل means *guide* in Arabic. The idea is simple. When a customer hands over a box, a prescription or a question, you should be able to find the product, see every alternative with the same ingredient and strength, and know how current that information is, in seconds and without a signal.

## What the app does

- Searches UAE medicines and pharmacy products by brand, generic name or ingredient, fully offline.
- Moves from a brand to its active ingredient and out to every product carrying the same ingredient and strength.
- Groups supplements by ingredient and salt form, so magnesium glycinate sits apart from magnesium oxide.
- Shows pregnancy and breastfeeding positions only where a published source supports them, with the citation beside the answer.
- Dates every record, so you can see how old the information is.

## How the blog is written

Articles on this site are researched from primary sources: peer-reviewed studies, clinical guidelines and official UAE health authority documents. Every factual claim links to its source, and each article lists its sources at the end. Where the evidence is weak or a rule could not be confirmed from an official source, the article says so rather than guessing.

Articles carry the date they were published and the date they were last reviewed. Regulatory rules in the UAE change often, so if you spot something out of date, please tell us.

## Independence

Daleel Rx is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of any UAE government authority, regulator, manufacturer or pharmacy group, and it carries no advertising or sponsored content.
