To practise as a pharmacist in Dubai you need a licence from the Dubai Health Authority (DHA), and you apply through its Sheryan portal. The route has five parts. You complete the free Sheryan self-assessment, have your documents verified by DataFlow, pass the Prometric computer-based exam unless you are exempt, and apply for "Get Registered". A DHA-licensed employer then activates your licence once you have a job offer. Below is each step in order, with the durations and fees DHA publishes as of September 2026.

For how DHA fits alongside the other three UAE regulators, start with our [overview of pharmacist licensing across MOH, DHA, DOH and SHA](/blog/uae-pharmacist-licensing-moh-dha-doh-sha/).

## Who can apply

Every UAE regulator licenses against the same Unified Healthcare Professional Qualification Requirements (PQR). DHA's [Manual for Licensing Healthcare Professionals v1.2](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/052025/Manual%20For%20Licensing%20Healthcare%20Professionals-Final202555984.pdf), effective 8 May 2025, makes meeting the PQR the second stage of its licensing pathway. That covers your qualifications, clinical experience, home-country licence and good standing.

DHA runs three separate pharmacy exams, listed in its [CBT assessment guideline](https://services.dha.gov.ae/sheryan/wps/contenthandler/!ut/p/digest!L_negPqVWa2FDiru8UDKPw/war/SheryanHomeThemeStatic/themes/Portal8.5/docs/Final_Online_Exam_Guideline_CBT.pdf) as Pharmacist, Clinical Pharmacy and Pharmacy Technician. Each title has its own PQR criteria. As a baseline, the Department of Health Abu Dhabi's [pharmacist scope of practice](https://www.doh.gov.ae/-/media/E160783B819C479D90E4DF8BAA108737.ashx) (February 2024) defines a pharmacist as holding at least a Bachelor of Pharmacy or an equivalent degree.

The experience years required for each qualification, and any country-based exam exemptions, sit in the PQR itself. Read the pharmacy chapter of the current [PQR hosted on Sheryan](https://services.dha.gov.ae/sheryan/wps/contenthandler/war/SheryanHomeThemeStatic/themes/Portal8.5/docs/PQR_April_2025.pdf) (the April 2025 edition at the time of writing) before you pay for anything.

Some histories make you ineligible under the manual. These include a licence revocation or suspension within the last 10 years, a warning within the last 2 years, an investigation in progress, blacklisting, or three failed exam attempts across UAE authorities.

## The steps in order

1. **Create a Sheryan account and run the self-assessment.** The self-assessment tool is free, according to the [DHA manual](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/052025/Manual%20For%20Licensing%20Healthcare%20Professionals-Final202555984.pdf). If it returns "ineligible", you can use the separate "review eligibility" service.

2. **Check your title's PQR requirements.** Confirm your qualification, experience and any exam exemption against the current PQR. DHA's [CBT guideline](https://services.dha.gov.ae/sheryan/wps/contenthandler/!ut/p/digest!L_negPqVWa2FDiru8UDKPw/war/SheryanHomeThemeStatic/themes/Portal8.5/docs/Final_Online_Exam_Guideline_CBT.pdf) says exam exemption is "based on the PQR criteria and DHA Manual for Licensing Healthcare Professionals".

3. **Submit your documents for primary source verification.** DHA delegates verification to a third-party agency. [DataFlow Group](https://dataflowgroup.com/organization/dubai-health-authority/) describes itself as DHA's verification partner since 2007, and applications go through the [DHA DataFlow portal](https://dha.dfgateway.com/). DHA does not publish the DataFlow fee or turnaround time, so check the portal when you apply.

4. **Book and sit the Prometric exam, unless you are exempt.** Per the [CBT guideline](https://services.dha.gov.ae/sheryan/wps/contenthandler/!ut/p/digest!L_negPqVWa2FDiru8UDKPw/war/SheryanHomeThemeStatic/themes/Portal8.5/docs/Final_Online_Exam_Guideline_CBT.pdf), you check the exam mode for your specialty, copy your DHA Unique ID from Sheryan, generate an Eligibility ID, then schedule with Prometric. The pharmacy exams are listed at USD 280. DHA's [Get Registered page](https://services.dha.gov.ae/sheryan/wps/portal/home/services-professional/service-description?scode=NRG&CATALOGUE_TYPE=PROFESSIONAL) states that you can run the DataFlow verification and the exam in parallel.

5. **Apply for "Get Registered".** Once you have a positive verification result and an exam pass (if required), you submit the [Get Registered](https://services.dha.gov.ae/sheryan/wps/portal/home/services-professional/service-description?scode=NRG&CATALOGUE_TYPE=PROFESSIONAL) application on Sheryan with a photo and passport copy. As of September 2026 the fee is AED 200, with "Knowledge and Innovation Fees" added at checkout, and processing time is stated as 5 working days. DHA may add an oral assessment "if required by position". On approval you are added to the Dubai Medical Registry and receive a Professional Registration certificate, the document many people still call the eligibility letter.

6. **Get a job offer and have your employer activate the licence.** Registration alone does not allow you to practise. A DHA-licensed facility activates your licence for one, two or three years, per the [DHA manual](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/052025/Manual%20For%20Licensing%20Healthcare%20Professionals-Final202555984.pdf). DHA does not publish the activation fee on the pages reviewed.

Registration is valid for one year from the date of issue (manual, Section 10.12). Plan your job search so that activation happens inside that year. DHA does not publish an end-to-end timeline, and the only processing time it states is the 5 working days for Get Registered.

## The exam itself

According to DHA's [CBT assessment guideline](https://services.dha.gov.ae/sheryan/wps/contenthandler/!ut/p/digest!L_negPqVWa2FDiru8UDKPw/war/SheryanHomeThemeStatic/themes/Portal8.5/docs/Final_Online_Exam_Guideline_CBT.pdf) (updated September 2026), the exams are computer-based and delivered by Prometric through a network of 8,000 test centres in more than 160 countries. The format is multiple-choice questions. The Pharmacist, Clinical Pharmacy and Pharmacy Technician exams are each listed at 150 questions, 3 hours and USD 280. The 3 hours include registration and the introduction at the test centre, so your actual answering time is shorter. Confirm these figures on Sheryan when you book, because DHA "reserves the right to" change the content, format or pass score "without an update in the content of this document".

**Results.** Sheryan shows "Pass" or "Fail". DHA does not share your score.

**Pass mark.** We do not quote a pass mark for the pharmacy exams. DHA's guideline gives pass scores between 45% and 70% across specialties and says DHA can change the pass score at any time.

**Attempts.** The [DHA manual](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/052025/Manual%20For%20Licensing%20Healthcare%20Professionals-Final202555984.pdf) (Section 7.3) gives you three attempts. Failing the third blocks you from reapplying for a DHA licence for two years. The count runs across all UAE regulators, so a failed MOHAP or DOH attempt uses one of your three. A fourth attempt is possible only with DHA approval and evidence of your failed attempts across UAE authorities. Failing to declare an attempt elsewhere is itself a ground for action (Section 5.26). DHA does not state a minimum waiting period between attempts.

**How long a pass lasts.** Under Sections 7.6 to 7.8 of the manual, you do not need a new assessment if you passed the DHA exam for the same title within the last 5 years and have no practice gap. The same applies if you cancelled a DHA, MOHAP or DOH licence within the last 5 years with no practice gap. Professionals with an active DHA registration are also exempt. A practice gap means more than 2 years out of clinical practice. If you have one and your pass is more than 2 years old, you must sit a new assessment.

## Primary source verification and the Good Standing Certificate

DataFlow checks your qualifications, experience letters, licences, Good Standing Certificate and any exam-exemption proof. The [manual](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/052025/Manual%20For%20Licensing%20Healthcare%20Professionals-Final202555984.pdf) (Section 6.2) lists three outcomes.

- **Positive.** You can proceed to Get Registered.
- **Unable to verify.** The source did not confirm the document. You may resubmit twice.
- **Discrepancy.** Something did not match. You may resubmit once.

A negative verification report leads to blacklisting (Section 5.27). Because the resubmission allowance is so small, check that names, dates and job titles on your experience letters match your other documents before you submit.

Your Good Standing Certificate must be issued by the authority that licensed you. It must be no more than 6 months old on the date you submit Get Registered (Section 10.3). A slow verification can leave it too old by the time you register.

## Fees DHA publishes

As of September 2026, these are the only DHA-related fees confirmed on official pages.

| Item | Fee | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sheryan self-assessment | Free | DHA manual v1.2 |
| Prometric exam (Pharmacist, Clinical Pharmacy, Pharmacy Technician) | USD 280 | DHA CBT guideline, Sep 2026 |
| Get Registered | AED 200 plus Knowledge and Innovation fees at checkout | DHA Get Registered page |
| DataFlow verification | Not published by DHA | Check the DataFlow portal |
| Licence activation | Not published on the pages reviewed | Confirm with your employer |
| Updating CPD points on Sheryan | Free | DHA Update CPD Points page |

Some consultancy sites quote much higher totals. Rely on the amount Sheryan or DataFlow shows at payment.

## DHA vs DOH vs MOHAP vs SHA

All four regulators apply the same PQR. The portals, exam vendors and published rules differ.

| | DHA (Dubai) | DOH (Abu Dhabi) | MOHAP | SHA (Sharjah) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Where you apply | Sheryan | Exam pre-booking fees paid in TAMM | MOHAP e-services; facility applies for the licence via UAE PASS | Facility applies on the SHA portal |
| Exam vendor | Prometric | Pearson VUE | Prometric | Not published |
| Published pass mark | Not published for pharmacy (45–70% range across specialties) | Not published | 60% | Not published |
| Attempts | 3 across all UAE authorities, then a two-year block | Not published | Three attempts permitted | Not published |
| Practice gap allowed | 2 years | Not published | 2 years | 3 years |
| Pharmacist licence fee | Not published (AED 200 registration) | Not published | AED 100 application, AED 1,000 licence | AED 1,000 full-time, AED 4,000 part-time |
| Stated processing time | 5 working days (Get Registered) | Not published | 5 working days (licence) | 8 working days |

Sources for this table are the [DHA manual](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/052025/Manual%20For%20Licensing%20Healthcare%20Professionals-Final202555984.pdf) and [Get Registered page](https://services.dha.gov.ae/sheryan/wps/portal/home/services-professional/service-description?scode=NRG&CATALOGUE_TYPE=PROFESSIONAL), [Pearson VUE's DOH page](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/doh.html), MOHAP's [evaluation service](https://mohap.gov.ae/en/w/evaluation-of-health-professional) and [pharmacist licensing service](https://mohap.gov.ae/en/w/licensing-of-pharmacists), and SHA's [professional licence service](https://sha.shj.ae/services_en/%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AE%D9%8A%D8%B5%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%87%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%8A%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D8%AD%D9%8A%D9%8A%D9%86%204_en.html). "Not published" means we did not find the figure on that regulator's official pages. The 60% pass mark is MOHAP's own figure and does not apply to the DHA exam. MOHAP's assessment certificate is valid for five years.

## Moving between emirates and the unified licensing platform

A UAE licence does not transfer automatically between regulators. DHA accepts PSV reports transferred from DOH, SHA and MOHAP (manual, Section 6.3), which can save you a second verification. If you hold a licence elsewhere in the UAE and want a higher title in Dubai, you must meet the PQR for that title and prove the other licence has been cancelled, and DHA may review the request (Section 5.33). SHA's own [transfer service](https://sha.shj.ae/services_en/%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AE%D9%8A%D8%B5%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%87%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%8A%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D8%AD%D9%8A%D9%8A%D9%86%2015_en.html) covers moves between Sharjah facilities only.

If you currently hold a MOH licence for Sharjah, read our guide to the [MOH to SHA transition in Sharjah](/blog/moh-to-sha-transition-sharjah/). For the wider federal changes, see [what changed at the UAE drug regulator](/blog/uae-drug-regulator-what-changed/).

In February 2025 MOHAP [announced a National Licensing Platform](https://mohap.gov.ae/en/w/mohap-launches-unified-national-platform-for-health-licenses-to-boost-healthcare-system-s-efficiency-and-competitiveness) intended to issue a standardised professional licence valid across the UAE, including for pharmacists. [Gulf News reported in August 2025](https://gulfnews.com/uae/health/uae-to-launch-unified-health-licensing-platform-in-2026-1.500224412) that full launch was planned for Q2 2026, with MOHAP, DOH, DHA and SHA taking part and mutual recognition of evaluations among the aims. We have not found official confirmation of how far it operates today. Check on Sheryan and with MOHAP whether it applies to your application before you assume a single licence covers you.

## CPD after you are licensed

DHA sets pharmacists a target of 20 CPD points a year. The [Sheryan CPD service page](https://services.dha.gov.ae/sheryan/wps/portal/home/services-professional/service-description?scode=CPD&CATALOGUE_TYPE=PROFESSIONAL) gives the totals by licence length as 20 points for a one-year licence, 40 for two years and 60 for three years. The [manual](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/052025/Manual%20For%20Licensing%20Healthcare%20Professionals-Final202555984.pdf) has a section quoting 80 and 120 points for multi-year licences, which matches the physician rate. The profession-specific Sheryan figures are consistent with 20 points a year, so follow those.

Under the manual (Sections 18.3 to 18.12), the rules are as follows.

- Your licence cannot be renewed until you meet the CPD requirement.
- At least 70% of your points must relate to your specialty. Up to 30% can be general.
- You can earn all your points through online programmes.
- Morning meetings, ward rounds and product training do not count.

Your employer applies for renewal within 3 months of expiry (Section 15.4). An expired licence can be renewed within 6 months with a late penalty, and you cannot practise while it is expired (Section 15.6). A licence is cancelled if you do not practise for 6 consecutive months without an acceptable reason (Section 15.7). If you work on a temporary permit, you cannot handle controlled, semi-controlled or narcotic medicines (Section 17.16).

## How to prepare sensibly

DHA's guideline does not publish a content blueprint or domain weighting for the pharmacy exams. It says content is written and validated by subject-matter experts and "updated on a frequent basis". You will not see a score after a failed attempt, so you get no feedback on weak areas.

- **Revise from everyday practice for your title.** DHA does not list topics, so work through therapeutics, dispensing, interactions, dosing and counselling using the standard clinical references you rely on at work.
- **Know the local framework.** The DHA manual lists Federal Law No. (6) of 2023 on the practice of pharmacists and non-physicians among its governing legislation. Read the licensing rules summarised above.
- **Practise under time.** You have 150 questions in 3 hours, and check-in comes out of that time.
- **Treat each attempt as scarce.** Three attempts across the UAE is a hard limit. Book when you are ready and avoid sites selling "recalled" exam questions.

## Questions people ask

**How much is the DHA exam fee for a pharmacist?**
DHA's CBT guideline lists USD 280 for the Pharmacist exam as of September 2026. Get Registered costs a further AED 200 plus Knowledge and Innovation fees. DataFlow charges separately.

**What is the DHA pass mark for pharmacists?**
DHA's guideline gives a 45–70% range across specialties and reserves the right to change the pass score. You receive only a pass or fail, never a score.

**Can I sit the exam before my DataFlow report comes back?**
Yes. DHA's Get Registered page says verification and the exam can run in parallel. You need both before you can register.

**Does a MOHAP or DOH exam pass exempt me from the DHA exam?**
DHA's manual confirms exemption for a DHA pass for the same title within 5 years, or a cancelled DHA, MOHAP or DOH licence within 5 years, each with no practice gap. It does not say a MOHAP or DOH pass alone exempts you. Confirm through the Sheryan self-assessment.

_DHA updates its requirements without much notice. Check Sheryan for the current rules before you pay any fee or book the exam._
