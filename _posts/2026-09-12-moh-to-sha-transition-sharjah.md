If you hold an MOH licence and work in Sharjah, something on your facility's compliance
checklist may have changed recently without anyone explaining why.

## What's actually happening

Sharjah Health Authority (SHA) has operated as an independent regulator since 2010,
separate from MOHAP, alongside DHA and DOH. Part of its remit is Sharjah Healthcare City
(SHCC) — a growing medical free zone, not a separate licensing authority in itself.

What's changed is scope. Several UAE healthcare licensing consultancies report that
facilities in Sharjah previously licensed under MOHAP are progressively moving under
SHA's own licensing framework instead. It isn't uniform — some clinics and hospitals stay
under MOHAP, others have moved or are moving to SHA oversight. Which category your
employer falls into determines whether this touches you at all.

## What it means if your facility is one of them

If your facility is transitioning, professionals who currently hold an MOH licence may
need to obtain an **SHA Assessment Certificate** before their licence can be activated or
renewed under SHA's system at that facility. In practice, that's a gate between your
existing MOH credential and continuing to practise there without a gap.

**Worth flagging plainly:** this is what licensing consultancies serving the sector are
reporting, not something I found in an official gazette notice. Treat it as a reason to
check, not as confirmed for your specific situation — ask your facility's HR or
compliance office directly whether you're affected, and if so, whether they're handling
the Assessment Certificate application or expecting you to.

## Why this matters beyond Sharjah

This is the same point as last week's post on the four licensing authorities, just moving
in real time: which authority governs your workplace isn't fixed forever. A facility can
shift jurisdiction, and when it does, your existing licence doesn't automatically follow
it. The practical habit worth building either way — check your facility's current
licensing authority directly rather than assuming it hasn't changed since you started.
